# Earth Shape Research Notes

## Scientific Consensus: Earth is an Oblate Spheroid

### Evidence for Round Earth:
1. **Gravitational forces** - Planets form spherical due to gravity pulling matter toward the center
2. **Observations from space** - Direct photographic evidence from satellites and astronauts
3. **Lunar eclipses** - Earth's shadow on the moon is always circular
4. **Horizon and ship masts** - Ships disappear hull-first over the horizon
5. **Time zones** - Different parts of Earth experience day/night simultaneously
6. **GPS and satellite navigation** - Requires spherical Earth model for accuracy
7. **Gravity measurements** - Varies predictably with latitude due to Earth's shape

### Flat Earth Theory Debunked:
- **Scientific method**: No empirical evidence supports flat Earth
- **Gravity explanation**: No working model explains gravity on a flat plane
- **Celestial observations**: Star patterns change predictably with latitude
- **Air travel routes**: Follow great circle routes (shortest path on sphere)
- **Satellite communications**: Would not work on flat Earth model

### Key Physics Principles:
- **Hydrostatic equilibrium**: Self-gravitating objects naturally form spheres
- **Minimum energy state**: Sphere has lowest potential energy configuration
- **Planetary formation**: Accretion disks collapse into spherical bodies
